# Weekly Content Calendar Template — @bryanclear

**How to use:** Copy this template each week. Fill in your own data/insights. Schedule on Buffer (2-3 weeks ahead).  
**Time per week:** ~10 minutes  
**Platforms:** X (thread format) → LinkedIn (reframe for business audience) → Instagram (screenshot best thread)

---

## WEEK OF [DATE]

### **MONDAY: The Problem (Thread)**

**Hook:**
> A $60B industry problem nobody's talking about: [Specific problem observation]

**Thread structure:**
1. Hook (above)
2. Why this matters
3. Real example or data point
4. Consequence/impact
5. Why it's still unsolved
6. The insight
7. End with question, not CTA

**Example:**
> A $60B industry problem nobody's talking about: Barber shops still don't know if clients will come back.
> 
> A barber executes a fade flawlessly. Client loves it. Barber thinks "nailed it." 
> 
> Client never comes back.
> 
> Barber has zero data on why. Was it the cut? The experience? Communication? The price?
> 
> Meanwhile the shop just lost a $500+ lifetime client (conservative estimate) and will never know why.

---

### **TUESDAY: Industry Data/Insight**

**Format:** Single powerful tweet with a stat or observation

**Template:**
> "[Stat/observation] and yet [consequence]. This is why [industry segment] is stuck."

**Examples:**
- "The barbershop industry is $60B+ globally. 85% of shops have <5 employees. How many use data to measure client satisfaction? Answer: almost none."
- "Fresha handles ~$XB in bookings for salons/barber shops. But they can't tell you if clients are satisfied. That's a gap worth solving."
- "Rob Peetoom runs 10+ salons with custom booking software. Most barber shops? WhatsApp groups and phone calls."

---

### **WEDNESDAY: Why Current Solutions Are Incomplete**

**Format:** Thread explaining the gap

**Hook:** "Why [existing solution] doesn't solve [the real problem]"

**Examples:**
- Why Fresha (SaaS booking) dominates but misses the consultation gap
- Why appointment books are still common (friction to switching)
- Why barbershops can't measure what matters (accuracy, satisfaction, retention)

---

### **THURSDAY: Solutions/Ideas**

**Format:** "What if..." thread

**Template:**
> "What if [small business] could [capability they lack]?"

**Examples:**
- "What if barbers could see which styles they nail vs which ones struggle with?"
- "What if clients could preview their exact look before sitting down (on their own hair, not a mannequin)?"
- "What if shop owners had one dashboard showing: client satisfaction, barber accuracy per style, retention rates?"

---

### **FRIDAY: Industry Dynamics Deep Dive**

**Format:** Observation or mini-analysis

**Examples:**
- How professional sports teams use data for athlete improvement; why barbers don't
- The gap between what clients expect and what barbers understand they want
- Why small business owners resist "process" (it feels like overhead)

---

### **SATURDAY: Short Take / Question**

**Format:** Single tweet asking a provocative question

**Examples:**
- "If a barber has 500 clients, how many could they describe in detail? Honest answer: maybe 20. Why?"
- "Why do barbershops remember client preferences better than they remember to invoice?"
- "How much revenue is lost because a client had ONE bad haircut and never came back?"

---

### **SUNDAY: Recap / Reflection**

**Format:** Meta-observation tying the week together

**Template:**
> "This week I kept coming back to [theme]. The pattern: [observation]. It matters because [why]."

**Example:**
> "This week I kept coming back to data gaps in small businesses. The pattern: owners run on intuition + memory, not systems. It matters because that's money on the table."

---

## HOW TO ADAPT THIS WEEKLY

**Each week, pick:**
- Monday: One real problem from your observations/conversations
- Tuesday: One stat or industry insight
- Wednesday: One solution that's incomplete
- Thursday: One "what if" idea
- Friday: One industry dynamic
- Saturday: One provocative question
- Sunday: Meta-reflection

**Then:**
1. Write the threads (30 min)
2. Create LinkedIn versions (reframe for business audience) (15 min)
3. Screenshot best thread for Instagram (5 min)
4. Schedule all on Buffer for next 2-3 weeks (10 min)

## LINKEDIN VERSION (Same Day, Different Framing)

Take Monday's thread and reframe:
- Lead with the business ops angle
- Add: "Why data-driven decision making matters"
- Professional tone
- Mention: "Thinking about solutions to this" (no CTA yet)

**Example for Monday's barber thread:**

> **The Hidden Cost of No Client Data**
> 
> Service businesses like barbershops operate on intuition. A barber executes a perfect fade, but has zero insight into whether the client will return.
> 
> No data on:
> - Why clients churn
> - Which styles drive repeat business
> - Accuracy per barber per style
> - Client satisfaction trends
> 
> The cost? Losing high-value repeat clients without knowing why.
> 
> Data-driven decision making isn't just for tech. It's how you scale any service business.

---

## INSTAGRAM (Screenshot Strategy)

Each week:
1. Pick the thread with strongest engagement/insight
2. Screenshot it from X
3. Post to Instagram with caption:
   > "This thread on [topic] resonated on X. [One-line summary]. Link in bio for more."

---

## TRACKING (Optional)

At end of each week, note:
- Which post got most engagement
- Which resonated most (replies, retweets)
- What you'd change next time

Don't obsess over metrics, but patterns matter (e.g., "data/stats get more traction than philosophy").
