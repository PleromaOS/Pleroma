# Privacy Policy

**Pleroma Consultation App**  
Last updated: July 2026

## 1. Overview

Pleroma is a consultation platform for barbershops. We help Clients preview their haircuts before they sit down, and help Barbers deliver exactly what Clients expect. This privacy policy explains how we handle your data.

## 2. Data We Collect

### For Clients
- **First name** (optional on first consultation)
- **Email address** (required to look up consultations on return visits)
- **Hair type, texture, skin tone** (consultation preferences)
- **Photographs** (optional, for AI clone generation in Personalized Consultations)
- **Feedback** (optional, after haircut: satisfaction rating and notes)

### For Barbers
- **Name** (linked to their profile and consultations)
- **PIN** (4-digit, used to authenticate to Staff View)

### For Shop Owners
- **Shop details** (name, location)
- **Access logs** (who accessed the dashboard, when)

## 3. How We Use Your Data

### Clients
- **To show you your consultation result** before you pay
- **To help your Barber understand your preferences** (Staff View)
- **To collect feedback** on how well your Barber delivered
- **To recognize you on return visits** (email lookup)
- **To measure Barber accuracy** (aggregate statistics, not personal targeting)

### Barbers
- **To authenticate you** to the Staff View (PIN)
- **To track your performance** (which styles you nail, which need improvement)
- **To manage your consultation queue** (real-time updates)

### Shop Owners
- **To show you Barber accuracy per style** (Admin Dashboard)
- **To measure Client satisfaction** (feedback trends)
- **To identify training opportunities** (where feedback disagrees with execution)

## 4. AI Clones & Biometric Data

### Optional & Consentable
- AI clones are **only generated with your explicit consent** on your first Personalized Consultation
- You must opt-in; we do not generate clones by default

### Original Photos Deleted Immediately
- We delete your original photos immediately after generating your AI clone
- Only the clone is stored in your Pleroma profile

### Clones Retained for Your Future Visits
- Your clone stays in your profile so you can compare styles on return visits
- You can delete your clone anytime, for any reason

### Clone Re-rendering
- When Pleroma improves its AI algorithm, we email you asking if you'd like your clone re-rendered
- Your choice: refresh with new algorithm, or keep the old one
- You control this decision

## 5. Your Rights & Control

### Access
You can view your consultations, feedback, and AI clone anytime by entering your email in the app.

### Deletion (Right to Be Forgotten)
You can request deletion of your profile anytime. When you do:

**Deleted immediately:**
- Your email address
- Your first name
- Your AI clone(s)
- Any PIN you set

**Anonymized & retained** (for operational & legal protection):
- Your consultation records (linked to "Deleted Client" instead of your name)
- Your feedback (linked to anonymous consultation, still helps Barbers improve)
- Staff can still reference what you requested ("Fade, tight sides") without knowing who you are

**Why we keep anonymized consultations:**
- Helps Barbers learn from past results
- Protects us if you later dispute what happened (we have proof of what you asked for)
- Complies with European record-keeping standards

**Hard deletion timeline:**
- Soft-deleted data is fully purged after 90 days
- You can contact your Shop directly if you want faster deletion

### Opting Out
- You can opt out of feedback emails anytime
- You can choose not to upload photos (use Quick Pick instead)

## 6. How We Store Your Data

### Secure Storage
- All data is stored in Supabase (EU servers)
- Data is encrypted at rest and in transit
- Access is restricted to authorized staff only

### No Third-Party Sharing
- We do **not** sell your data
- We do **not** share with Fresha, booking systems, or other services
- Your data stays in Pleroma unless you explicitly choose to share it

### AI Processing
- Photos uploaded for AI clone generation are sent to third-party AI services (e.g., Higgsfield, Banana)
- We use these services only to generate your clone
- Original photos are deleted from these services after processing
- These services are bound by our data processing agreements

This privacy policy complies with GDPR and ePrivacy Regulations.

**Your rights under GDPR:**
- Right to access your data
- Right to rectification (correct inaccurate data)
- Right to erasure (right to be forgotten, described above)
- Right to restrict processing
- Right to data portability
- Right to object to processing
- Right to lodge complaints with your local data protection authority

**To exercise these rights:**
Contact your Shop Owner, or email us directly at [contact email — TBD].

## 8. Multi-Shop & Data Isolation

Pleroma is built for multiple barbershops.

**Important:** Your data is scoped to each Shop. If you use the same email at two different Shops, you have **separate Client profiles** at each Shop. Each Shop only sees their own Clients and Consultations — never data from other Shops.

**This means:**
- Your Consultation at Shop A is invisible to Shop B
- Each Shop Owner only sees their own Barbers and Clients
- Your data is never pooled across Shops

## 9. Data Retention

### How Long We Keep Data

- **Active consultations**: Retained indefinitely (operational & historical reference)
- **Deleted client data**: Soft-deleted immediately, hard-deleted after 90 days
- **Feedback**: Retained indefinitely (helps Barber training)
- **Access logs**: Retained for 1 year (security audit trail)
- **AI clones**: Retained until you delete them or close your account

### Why We Retain Consultations After Deletion
- Barbers reference past consultations to understand your style preferences
- Helps us defend against disputes ("You asked for a tight fade, we delivered")
- Complies with GDPR lawful basis (legitimate business interest in dispute resolution)

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy over time. We'll notify you of material changes via email (if we have your email) or by displaying a notice in the app.
