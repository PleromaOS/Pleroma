looks at you. / 2. We read your hair. You tap yes or no. / 3. Choose your cut.
A famous style, or your own. / 4. Your AI twin. You on a good day, not in
bathroom light, so you judge the cut and not the lighting. You check it looks
like you. / 5. Your new cut, on you, plus a brief for your barber. / Every
hair type, straight to coily. / [Next]
