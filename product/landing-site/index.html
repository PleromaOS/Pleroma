The problem

The churn
you can't see.

A client stops coming in. You don't know if they were unhappy, moved away, or just got busy. Your business has no way to tell the difference — and no way to fix what you can't measure.

The knowledge
you don't own.

Your best stylist knows exactly what 30 clients want — the length, the texture, the colour they're sensitive about. That knowledge lives in their head. Not in your business.

The loyalty that
was never yours.

When they leave, the clients go with them. You built the space, the brand, the reputation. The relationships? Those always belonged to one person.

Simple by design

Three steps.
Under a minute.

No app. No login. No training needed. Clients figure it out on their own.

1
Scan

Client scans the QR code

In your waiting area, on your booking confirmation, or via WhatsApp. Opens instantly in any browser — no app needed, no account required.

2
Choose

60 seconds of guided questions

On screen the moment the client sits down. Clear, precise, ready to act on. Your business keeps the record — forever, even if the stylist leaves.
