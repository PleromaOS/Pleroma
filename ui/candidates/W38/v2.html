Your face. Your rules.

  • We never sell your data.

    Not to advertisers, not to anyone. We earn from the shops that use PleromaOS, not from you.

  • Seen only by your barber.

    Your barber at Barber Jansen, and the secure services that make this work: our storage and the AI that draws your cut, on our instructions. Nobody else.

  • Stored in Europe.

    Your photos and details are kept on servers in Ireland, under European privacy law.

  • Your AI twin, only if you say yes.

    At the end we ask if you want to keep it for next time. Say no and it is deleted with your photos.

  • Stop anytime.

    Nothing is saved until you give your email. Close the page before that and your photos are gone.

  • Delete it all, whenever you want.

    Email bryan@pleromaos.nl and we delete your photos, your twin and your details.

  • 16 or older.

    Face photos of anyone under 16 need a parent's permission, which this consultation can't ask for.
