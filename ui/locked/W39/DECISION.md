# W39 · Introduction · the guarantee — LOCKED

**Variant 2 (the seal) · locked 27 September 2026 by Bryan** ("I like number 2").

## What won

> One "Guaranteed" seal stays on screen and earns a gold mark per story
> slide: the client's check, the barber's check, then "14". By the last slide
> the seal is complete.

Slides: A promise, not just words (if it doesn't match your brief, you don't
pay) → Checked together (you confirm, your barber confirms before cutting) →
Say it in the chair (before you leave; valid 14 days). Ends with **I'm ready**
and "Next: get ready for your scan".

## Why

The seal shows that the result guarantee is built from two confirmations and
a time limit, not a slogan, and one object filling up echoes the welcome
screen's ring. Same stories chassis as W37 and W38.

## What the rejected variant got wrong

- **1** (same chassis, a new picture per slide) was consistent but made the
  guarantee three separate pictures instead of one thing being completed.

## Constraints

- Glossary words only: "result guarantee", never "money-back". Claim window =
  in the chair before leaving; offer validity = 14 days (CONTEXT.md).
- The seal's marks follow the visible slide (going back removes a mark).
