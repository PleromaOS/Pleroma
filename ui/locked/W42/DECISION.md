# W42 · Finding screen — LOCKED (anchor of the scan path)

**Variant 4 (conversation) · locked 27 September 2026 by Bryan** ("I like number 4").

## What won

> Each finding arrives as a message: a small round crop of the client's own
> photo, zoomed to the area, beside a bubble with the label (TEXTURE, COLOUR,
> HAIRLINE…) and the reading, e.g. "Your hair looks **wavy**". Two full-width
> answers under it: **Yes, that's right** (gold) and **Not quite**.

**Not quite** opens that item's answers on the same screen ("Which is
closer?"), with the AI's reading marked "our reading"; tapping one moves on.
**Yes** moves straight on. A counter (1 / 10) shows where you are.

## Why

The softest tone of the seven, which matters for the sensitive findings
(hairline, thinning): a reading offered in conversation, not a verdict. It
reads like being talked to by someone who looked carefully.

## What the rejected variants got wrong

- **1** spotlight, **7** ring progress: strong visuals, but a bare statement
  over a zoomed photo feels like a diagnosis for the hairline and thinning.
- **2** swipe card: fast, but swiping a stranger's reading of your hairline
  away is the wrong gesture for a careful confirmation.
- **3** pointed out: shows WHERE we looked; worth borrowing later if clients
  ask "what is this based on?".
- **5** billboard: fastest to read, but loud: "a little higher at the
  temples" in 64px type is exactly the wrong emphasis.
- **6** brief builds: good trust idea (see your brief being written); could
  return as the "Reading your hair" or summary screen.

## Constraints

- Wording: "Yes, that's right" / "Not quite" (the spec said Yes / No; the
  conversational labels replace them on this widget).
- No full stop after the reading: when words arrive one by one, a trailing
  full stop wrapped onto its own line.
- A finding the AI is not sure about is asked as a normal question, not shown
  as a message (scan-path.md decision 3).
- The example findings are placeholders; the real ones come from the analyser
  (not built yet).
