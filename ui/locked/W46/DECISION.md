# W46 · Conversation question · word answers — LOCKED

**Variant 4 (stacked with drawings) · locked 27 September 2026 by Bryan** ("Let's go with 4").

## What won

> One reusable piece for every "pick one of a few words" question in the
> conversation. The assistant asks in a bubble; the answers appear under
> the thread as full-width cards, each with a small drawing, the answer and
> a one-line hint. The tap turns the card gold, then the answer appears as
> the client's reply bubble and the next question follows.

Same card edge, radius, gold pressed state and reply bubble as the locked
picture replies (W03 v5), so words and pictures read as one conversation.

## Why

The drawing is read before the words, so the client barely has to read
(Bryan's "least thinking" rule), and it is the closest to the picture
replies without needing a photo.

## What the rejected variants got wrong

- **1** a row to swipe: most consistent with the picture row, but words are
  compared, and the third answer sat half off screen.
- **2** stacked cards: clear and safe; it is what variant 4 falls back to
  for an answer that has no drawing yet.
- **3** grid of two: shorter to scan, but uneven card heights and an odd
  card out.

## Constraints

- Every answer needs its own small drawing: effort uses the one-to-three
  upkeep bars (the W02 meter), the neckline shows the edge itself. A
  question without drawings shows the same cards without the drawing
  square (variant 2's look); nothing breaks.
- **Barber's choice** is never a card: always last, a plain line with no
  fill, and only on fade height, fade shape and neckline
  (DESIGN-SYSTEM.md 7.16).
- No typing dots before these questions (decision 13): dots only for real
  work (reading, drawing).
- The hint is one line on a 390px phone; cut the copy rather than shrink
  the type.
- "Run the demo again" is demo-only.
