# W41 · Reading your hair — LOCKED

**Variant 3 (narrated) · locked 27 September 2026 by Bryan** ("my pick is number 3").

## What won

> The reading is told as a short text conversation: "Great photos. Thanks."
> → "Looking at the front…" → "Checking both sides…" → "Almost there…",
> each its own small message with typing dots between them, then the first
> finding lands as a message with **Yes, that's right** / **Not quite** and
> the conversation continues in the finding screen (W42).

## States

- **Slow** (reading takes longer than expected, roughly 20 s; to be tuned):
  "This is taking a little longer than usual. Hang on." Dots keep going.
- **Can't read:** "We couldn't read your photos clearly. Answer a few quick
  questions instead, or scan again in better light." → **Answer questions**
  (switches to the question path) or **Scan again**.

## Why

Most alive, most like texting; it makes the wait itself the start of the
conversation the finding screen continues.

## What the rejected variants got wrong

- **1** (pure conversation): calm but the wait shows only dots, no sense of
  what is happening.
- **2** (photos being read): Claude's recommendation, as proof the reading is
  of the client's own photos; its photo strip could be borrowed later if
  clients doubt the reading.

## Constraints

- **Honest note:** the analyser returns once, at the end. The step messages
  advance on a timer so the screen feels alive; only the final answer is
  real (same approach as the render wait).
- The small round photo beside each message is the client's own photo.
- The Normal / Slow / Can't read buttons in the candidate file are for the
  demo only; they are not part of the widget.
