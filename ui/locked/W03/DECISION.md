# W03 · Route chooser — LOCKED

**Variant 5 (conversation with picture replies) · locked 27 September 2026 by Bryan** ("5 is the one").

## What won

> In the same chat as the findings: "Now the fun part." → "Pick a cut you
> like, or build your own." The reply area is a row of cuts the client
> swipes and taps, filtered to their hair type ("These suit wavy hair.").
> "See all styles" opens the full list; **Build my own, piece by piece** is
> one button underneath. The pick appears as the client's reply, with its
> photo.

Tapping a cut chooses the route AND the cut in one go (route A). "Build my
own" starts route B (top, sides, beard, piece by piece).

## Why

Built on decision 13 (scan-path.md): the introduction tells a story, then
everything from the scan to the booking is one conversation, because texting
feels familiar and a third change of style mid-way breaks the experience.
Bryan liked the conversation (variant 1); variant 3's idea (the cuts ARE the
choice, least thinking) was kept by putting the cuts inside the chat as
picture replies.

## What the rejected variants got wrong

- **1** in the conversation: right style, but the client first has to
  understand two abstract routes before seeing a single haircut.
- **2** two picture cards: clearest two-way choice, but a full-screen page
  that leaves the conversation.
- **3** the cuts are the choice: least thinking, but a full-screen page that
  leaves the conversation. Its idea lives on in 5.
- **4** three routes: added "Keep my style, just fresh", which is not in the
  spec. Not chosen; still worth considering as a future route, because many
  clients just want their usual cut done well.
